01 · Profile

About

I'm an IoT and Robotics Engineering undergraduate at the University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh, currently completing my final year while working as a Trainee IoT Engineer at SmartData Technologies Ltd.

My work sits at the intersection of embedded hardware and intelligent software — sensor calibration and PCB design on one side, computer vision and machine learning on the other. I've built systems ranging from autonomous aerial surveillance to smart-agriculture platforms deployed with real field sensors.

Outside coursework and projects, I serve as Organizing Secretary of the UFTB Robotics Club, and my research on aerial surveillance and industrial IoT has been recognised with a Best Paper Award and publication in an IEEE conference and an international journal.
